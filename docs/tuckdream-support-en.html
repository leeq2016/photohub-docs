Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does DreamJar upload my dreams to the cloud?

A: No. DreamJar stores all dream content (text, voice, photos) locally on your device. No account, no cloud sync, no data ever leaves your phone.

Q: Why does DreamJar need microphone access?

A: Only when you tap the record button to log a voice dream. The recording stays on your device as an .m4a file and is never uploaded.

Q: Why does DreamJar ask for speech recognition permission?

A: When you choose to convert your spoken dream into text, iOS uses its on-device speech recognizer. The audio is processed locally and never sent to a server.

Q: How do I delete a dream?

A: Long-press the jar on the main screen and confirm deletion. This permanently removes the dream along with its voice recording and attached photos.

Q: What happens to my dreams if I uninstall the app?

A: All local data (text, voice, photos, settings) is permanently deleted. We cannot restore it because we never had it. Please back up your device via iCloud or a computer before uninstalling.

Q: How do I restore my purchase after reinstalling or switching devices?

A: Open the App, go to About → "Unlock Full Version" → "Restore Purchase". Your lifetime unlock will be re-applied automatically through your Apple ID.

Q: I bought the Lifetime Unlock but the skins still show as locked. What should I do?

A: Tap "Restore Purchase" on the About page. If it still doesn't work, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase, then email us.

Q: Can I request a refund?

A: Yes. Refunds for in-app purchases are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com within 14 days (EU/UK consumers) or per your local consumer rights. EU/UK consumers have a 14-day right of withdrawal — see our Terms of Use Section 6.

Q: How many dreams can I record?

A: There is no artificial limit. The App stores dreams locally via SwiftData — the only constraint is your device's available storage.

Q: Does DreamJar support iPhone and iPad?

A: Yes, DreamJar supports both iPhone and iPad running iOS 17 or later.

Q: What languages does DreamJar support?

A: Simplified Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, German, French, and Arabic.

Q: Why do some jar skins show a lock icon?

A: Those are paid skins (certain crystal jars and all porcelain jars). Tapping a locked skin opens the purchase page where you can unlock everything with a one-time purchase.
