BeadSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 1, 2026

Core Promise: BeadSnap is a fully offline application. We do not collect, transmit, or share any user data.

1. Overview

BeadSnap is a photo-to-bead-pattern converter app. We take your privacy seriously and this policy explains how we handle your information.

2. Information Collection

This app does not collect any user data.

  • No personal identification information is collected
  • No photos are uploaded or transmitted
  • No location data is accessed
  • No cookies or tracking technologies are used
  • No data is shared with any third parties
  • No advertising or analytics SDKs are included
  • No internet connection is required

3. Data Access

This app requires access to the following data to provide its core functionality:

Data TypePurposeStorage
Photo library (read-only)Convert photos into bead patternsLocal device only
App project filesSave and restore bead projects with progressLocal device only

All data processing happens entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

4. Data Storage

BeadSnap stores project data locally on your device using the iOS Application Support directory. This includes:

  • Saved bead pattern projects (including thumbnail images and compressed originals)
  • Bead placement progress tracking data

Deleting the app will permanently remove all locally stored data.

5. Third-Party Services

This app does not use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics tools (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • Advertising networks
  • Social media SDKs
  • Cloud storage services
  • Crash reporting tools

6. Children's Privacy

BeadSnap is suitable for all ages. It does not collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.
