FAQ

Q: Does PhotoHub collect my photo data?

A: No. PhotoHub runs 100% locally on your device. All data processing happens on your iPhone/iPad, and no data is ever uploaded.

Q: Why does PhotoHub need photo library access?

A: PhotoHub needs to read photo metadata (dates) to generate the heatmap and display thumbnails for browsing.

Q: Can deleted photos be recovered?

A: Yes. Deleted photos are moved to the system "Recently Deleted" album, where you can restore them within 30 days.

Q: Does PhotoHub support iPhone and iPad?

A: Yes, PhotoHub supports both iPhone and iPad devices.

Q: What is Quick Select?

A: Quick Select is a card-swiping interface that shows your photos one by one. Swipe up to select, swipe left or right to skip — a fast way to filter through large batches of photos.

Q: What is the "Snap" feature?

A: The Snap feature randomly selects about half of your photos from a given day, helping you quickly decide which ones to keep. Just like snapping your fingers!

Q: How do I select all screenshots at once?

A: Enter selection mode, then tap the filter button at the top and choose "Select All Screenshots" to quickly select all screenshots from that day.
