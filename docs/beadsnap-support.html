❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does BeadSnap collect my photos?

A: No. BeadSnap processes everything locally on your device. Your photos are never uploaded anywhere.

Q: Why does it need photo library access?

A: BeadSnap needs to read the photo you select in order to convert it into a bead pattern. No other photos are accessed.

Q: What bead brands are supported?

A: BeadSnap supports MARD (马得), Perler, and generic brand color palettes with 34 standard colors.

Q: Can I save my progress?

A: Yes! You can save projects and resume later. The app tracks your bead placement progress section by section.

Q: What's the maximum grid size?

A: Grid sizes range from 16 to 256, giving you flexibility from small keychains to large wall displays.

Q: Does it work on iPhone and iPad?

A: Yes, BeadSnap supports both iPhone and iPad devices.
