Everything You Need to Reclaim Your Storage

Powerful tools to see, select, and clean your photo library.

Photo Heatmap

See your entire photo library at a glance with a GitHub-style heatmap calendar. Darker squares mean more photos — instantly spot the days you went photo-crazy.

Solves: "I have no idea how many photos I take each day"

Thanos Snap

Can't decide what to delete? One tap randomly selects half your photos. It's the fastest way to break through decision paralysis and start cleaning.

Solves: "I stare at my photos for 10 minutes and still can't decide"

Quick Select

A Tinder-like card-swiping experience for your photos. Swipe up to select for deletion, swipe sideways to skip. Filter hundreds of photos in minutes.

Solves: "Scrolling through thousands of photos one by one is painful"

Batch Select Screenshots

Screenshots quietly eat up gigabytes of storage. One tap selects every screenshot from a day — review and delete them all at once.

Solves: "I have 3 GB of screenshots I never look at"

Personalized Photo Reports

Beautiful data visualizations of your photo habits — busiest month, daily averages, longest streak, and your "photographer personality". Generate stunning annual and monthly report cards with real heatmap visuals, then share them on social media.

Solves: "I want to understand my photo habits and share my year in photos"

11 Stunning Themes PRO

Make it yours. Choose from 11 hand-crafted color themes — including 4 premium themes with immersive 3D light & shadow effects. From classic green to ocean blue to jade and peach. Your photos, your style.

Solves: "I want my photo tool to look as good as my photos"

Day Markers PRO

Mark the moments that matter. Use 8 unique shapes — heart, star, camera, and more — to highlight birthdays, trips, milestones, or any day worth remembering right on your heatmap.

Solves: "I want to remember which days were special, not just which had the most photos"

Peak Day Animations PRO

Your busiest photo days deserve to stand out. The highest-activity days pulse with a gentle breathing animation — a living heatmap that tells your story at a glance. Combined with premium themes, your calendar comes alive.

Solves: "I want to instantly spot my most memorable days on the calendar"
